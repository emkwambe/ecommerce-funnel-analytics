# Adversarial Review — Analysis B (were carted products purchased in a later session?)

Protocol: verified-analytics-project v2.1, `references/adversarial-review.md`. Consequential risk (the sprint brief), so the full review applies, with independent paths wherever they exist. Claims reviewed: C8–C15 in `ai-workflow/claim-ledger.md`. Evidence: `ai-workflow/evidence/sprint-2/later_purchases.json`, `verify.json`, and `web/public/data/investigation_later_purchases.json`. Fields are cited instead of typed figures (CLAUDE.md rule 1).

The review was run by Claude Code, the same model that built the analysis. That is the weakest reviewer; the independent paths below (R2 by a second implementation, the pre-committed stop rules, and the owner's H4 decision and H9 review) carry the weight.

| Attack (brief, Step 5 item 5, and the protocol table) | How it was tested | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity linking**: does `user_id` link the same person across sessions? | Identity checks on the B1 population (`checks[metric_group=user_id_check]`), recomputed independently (`B-all:user_id_check:*`); the stop rule | Within-session identity holds: no missing or multiple `user_id` (C15). **Cross-session identity cannot be tested** in this file.
