**Operational details fixed before the real run** (Claude Code's reading of the approved record where it left a choice; flagged for owner review, and any change after results is a deviation):
- **Rank intervals** are the 5th and 95th percentiles of the resampled ranks (`inverted_cdf`, so the endpoints are ranks that occurred). Ties share the best rank.
- **The ranked set** of each specification comes from its own point data (category-sessions ≥ the minimum). It is fixed across that specification's resamples.
- **The prior-precision cap** is s = a + b ≤ 1e10. At the cap there is no overdispersion, and the prior is the pooled rate.
- **Levels:** the sensitivities (item 12) run at both levels. Item 10 applies items 1–9 to the drill-down and doesn't say where item 12 applies, and running at both levels is the superset. C5 at the top level is reported as "same ranked set as C1" without a rerun when the ranked set doesn't change.
- **F2 (prior collapse),** where the record gives no number: "tight" = every ranked category's shrinkage weight s/(n + s) above 0.99; "loose" = all of them below 0.001.
- **F5:** the number of ranked categories whose set of separable partners differs between B and C (Wilson), with a limit of 2.
- **F7:** the rank-1 category's category-sessions below the median of the ranked categories.
- **F8:** under C5 and C8 only. The categories ranked in both specifications are re-ranked within that common set; the rule fires on a move of more than 2 places, or on any common pair whose separable status differs.
- **The run order** is: every specification first, then the F rules. `funnel.rankings` exits 3 if any rule fired, and nothing is interpreted before the escalation brief.
